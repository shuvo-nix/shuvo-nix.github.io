Usability Testing on a Budget: 5 Users Is Enough

Jul 2026·5 min read

Teams skip usability testing because they picture a lab, a recruiter, and a five figure invoice. The reality is far simpler. Decades of research back the idea that five users uncover the vast majority of usability problems, and you can run those sessions with a laptop and a video call.

My 60 Minute Session Format

  • Warm up (5 min): explain there are no wrong answers, you are testing the product, not them.
  • Three tasks (40 min): realistic goals, phrased as scenarios. Find a plan that fits a team of ten, not click Pricing.
  • Think aloud: ask them to narrate what they expect before every click. Expectations are where the insights live.
  • Probe gently: when they hesitate, ask what they are looking for. Never rescue them too early.
  • Wrap (10 min): ask what nearly stopped them and what surprised them. Write down exact quotes.

What to Do With the Findings

After five sessions, patterns are obvious. Group the friction points by frequency and severity, then fix the top three before you even think about testing again. Small loops beat big studies, because every round of fixes makes the next round of testing more valuable.

Five users, one hour each, zero excuses. Your future customers will thank you.
