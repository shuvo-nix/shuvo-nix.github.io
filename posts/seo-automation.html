SEO Automation: Rank While You Sleep

Aug 2026·5 min read

SEO has a reputation for being slow, manual, and mysterious. The slow part is real, rankings take months to build, but the manual part is optional. Most of the repetitive SEO work on a small site can be automated without touching anything shady.

What I Automate

  • Meta checks: a scheduled crawl flags missing titles, duplicate descriptions, and pages that lost their meta tags after an update.
  • Internal link suggestions: when a new post publishes, a script surfaces older posts it should link to, based on shared keywords.
  • Content briefs: keyword research feeds into a structured brief template, so writing starts with intent, headings, and questions already mapped.
  • Rank tracking: positions for target keywords get logged weekly into a dashboard, with alerts when a page drops more than five spots.
  • Image alt audits: a crawler lists every image missing alt text, which helps both accessibility and image search.

What I Never Automate

Judgment. Automation is excellent at telling you that a page dropped or that a title is missing. It is terrible at deciding what your audience actually needs to read next. Content strategy, final edits, and anything that touches your brand voice stay human. The machine handles the monitoring, you handle the meaning.

Set up the pipes once, and SEO stops being a monthly panic and becomes a quiet background process.
