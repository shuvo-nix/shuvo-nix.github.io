Nomad Coffee Co. - Brand Identity

Client

Nomad Coffee Co.

Role

Brand & Digital Design

Timeline

3 weeks

Year

2026

Overview

Nomad Coffee Co. is a specialty roastery built around travel: every roast is named after a route, a city, or a journey. They had great coffee and a great story, but a look that could have belonged to anyone.

The Challenge

Specialty coffee shelves are crowded with minimalist kraft bags and sans serif wordmarks. Nomad needed to stand out without alienating the wholesale buyers who put them on those shelves in the first place.

The Approach

  • Ran story workshops with the founders to pin down what travel means to the brand beyond the label names.
  • Designed a wordmark and compass inspired mark that works embossed on a bag or tiny on a social avatar.
  • Built a warm, map toned palette and a two font system with clear hierarchy rules.
  • Directed packaging layout templates so new roasts slot in without a designer each time.
  • Delivered a one page brand sheet plus launch assets: social templates, pitch deck, and press kit.

The Results

  • Secured two retail listings within the first month of the rebrand.
  • Launch content ran across four channels with a single consistent look.
  • The team ships new roast labels in house using the templates.
