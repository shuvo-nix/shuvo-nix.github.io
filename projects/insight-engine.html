Overview

Insight Engine is a SaaS team whose product generates a constant stream of usage data. Every week, someone exported CSVs, cleaned them in a spreadsheet, and assembled a report by hand. It took six hours, every single week.

The Challenge

The manual process was not just slow, it was fragile. Numbers were copied wrong, weeks were skipped when the usual person was away, and by the time leadership read the report the data was already stale.

The Approach

  • Mapped every data source feeding the report and documented the cleanup rules that lived in one person's head.
  • Built an n8n pipeline that pulls, cleans, and aggregates the data automatically.
  • Designed a live analytics dashboard answering the ten questions leadership actually asks.
  • Added a Slack digest every Monday at 8am and automatic alerts when a metric moves abnormally.

The Results

  • The six hour weekly report now takes zero hours.
  • Data is current to the hour instead of stale by a week.
  • Anomaly alerts caught two real incidents in the first month that would previously have gone unnoticed.
