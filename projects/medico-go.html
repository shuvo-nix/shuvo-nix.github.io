Medico Go - Health App Testing

Client

Medico Go

Role

QA & Testing

Timeline

8 weeks

Year

2026

Overview

Medico Go helps patients book appointments, store prescriptions, and message their doctors. Before public launch, the team needed confidence that an app handling sensitive health data would not fall over in real users' hands.

The Challenge

Health apps live and die on trust, and the edge cases are brutal: expired sessions mid-booking, weak connections in clinics, accessibility needs that are not optional for this audience. Two codebases, iOS and Android, doubled the surface area.

The Approach

  • Wrote 400+ structured test cases in QA Touch, covering booking, prescriptions, messaging, and auth.
  • Built a device matrix spanning budget Android phones to the latest iPhones, all real hardware.
  • Designed a regression suite the team now re-runs before every release.
  • Triaged every bug in Jira with severity, reproduction steps, and screen recordings.
  • Ran accessibility passes on contrast, focus order, and screen reader labels throughout.

The Results

  • 60+ critical and major bugs caught and fixed before a single public user saw them.
  • Launch builds hold a 99.7% crash free session rate.
  • Both app stores approved the app on first submission.
