Finlytic - Fintech Dashboard Redesign

Client

Finlytic

Role

UX Research & UI Design

Timeline

6 weeks

Year

2026

Overview

Finlytic gives small finance teams a real time view of company spending. The product was powerful but intimidating: twelve top level nav items, dense tables everywhere, and a setup flow that lost most trial users before they ever saw their own data.

The Challenge

Session recordings showed users opening the dashboard, scrolling once, and leaving. Interviews confirmed it: people could not answer their most basic question, where is our money going this month, without exporting to a spreadsheet. The tool had the data and buried the answer.

The Approach

  • Interviewed 8 users across 5 companies and mapped the five questions they actually ask weekly.
  • Rebuilt the information architecture around those questions, collapsing twelve nav items into four.
  • Designed a card based summary layer above the tables, so answers come first and detail follows.
  • Delivered a lean design system in Figma covering tokens, tables, charts, and empty states.
  • Ran two rounds of usability tests on the prototype before a single screen was built.

The Results

  • Task completion time on core reports dropped by 40% in follow up testing.
  • Trial to activation rate rose 24% within the first month after launch.
  • Support tickets tagged as navigation confusion fell by half.
