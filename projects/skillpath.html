Skillpath - SaaS Onboarding Flow

Client

Skillpath

Role

UX/UI & UX Writing

Timeline

5 weeks

Year

2026

Overview

Skillpath helps companies train new hires with structured learning paths. The product delivered once you were inside, but 60% of new accounts never completed a single meaningful action. The first mile was losing everyone.

The Challenge

The original setup asked for everything up front: company details, team structure, course preferences, branding. Eighteen fields before a user saw anything work. Analytics showed the drop-off cliff exactly at that form.

The Approach

  • Mapped the funnel step by step and cut setup to the three inputs that change what the user sees.
  • Introduced progressive disclosure: advanced settings moved to after the first win, a published sample path.
  • Rewrote every label, helper text, and empty state with microcopy focused on the user's goal, not the database.
  • Built an interactive prototype and tested it with 6 target users in Maze before development.

The Results

  • Activation, defined as publishing a first learning path, rose 24%.
  • Setup abandonment dropped by 18 percentage points.
  • Median time to first published path was cut roughly in half.
