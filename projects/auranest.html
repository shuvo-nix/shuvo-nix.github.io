Auranest - E-commerce Storefront

Client

Auranest

Role

Design & Development

Timeline

4 weeks

Year

2026

Overview

Auranest sells minimalist home goods to a young, mobile first audience. Their old storefront loaded slowly, looked generic, and leaked customers at every step of checkout. They needed a rebuild that felt like the brand and sold like one too.

The Challenge

Analytics told the story: 78% of traffic arrived on phones, yet mobile bounce rate was the worst of any channel. Product pages took nearly five seconds to become usable, and the checkout asked for information the store did not even need.

The Approach

  • Rebuilt the storefront mobile first, with compressed imagery and a performance budget from day one.
  • Designed product pages around photography and reviews, moving specs into clean accordions.
  • Simplified checkout to the essentials and added express payment options.
  • Integrated a CMS so the team updates collections and banners without touching code.
  • Passed WCAG basics: contrast, focus states, labels, and full keyboard checkout.

The Results

  • Page load dropped from 4.8s to 1.9s on mobile connections.
  • Mobile conversion rate rose 32% in the first six weeks.
  • Bounce rate on product pages fell by 21%.
