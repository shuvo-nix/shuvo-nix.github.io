Find quick answers to commonly asked questions about my services.

How quickly can you start?
Once we have had our kick-off call, we can get started on the same day. Send your request in and we will get you up and running on your project right away.
What if I do not like the designs?
We are easy going and can correct course quickly. We work with you and your team to make sure we get to the right solution as fast as possible. Revisions are part of the process.
Why not just hire a full-time designer?
Good question. Full-time designers are expensive to hire, onboard, and retain. With ShuvoniX you get senior-level output, flexible hours, and no overhead. You only pay for what you need, when you need it.
How much design work will I get?
I work with you to ensure deliverables are sent in a timely manner. Output depends on the size and complexity of your requests. We scope everything upfront so expectations are clear from day one.
What software do you use?
For design and prototyping: Figma, Adobe XD, Framer, and Balsamiq. For project management: Jira and other Atlassian tools. For QA: QA Touch, Maze, and Lookback. For automation: n8n. For research and documentation: Miro, Whimsical, Notion, and Google Forms.
Do you offer long-term support?
Yes. I keep a close relationship with clients after launch and am available for ongoing design, development, or QA support as your product evolves.
